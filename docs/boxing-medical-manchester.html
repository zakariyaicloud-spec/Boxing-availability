What is a BCR1 medical?

It's the medical every amateur boxer needs before they can compete with England Boxing. An England Boxing-registered doctor checks your health, eyesight, heart, lungs and nervous system, then records the result on England Boxing's system. It needs renewing every 12 months.

What do I need before my medical?

England Boxing membership and age verification done on The Locker. Without them the doctor can't record your result. Bring photo ID, your glasses or contact lenses if you wear them, and a list of any medication and past injuries.
