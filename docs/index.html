How payment works

You pay by bank transfer, so there are no card fees. Your money goes straight on to the clinic.

Booking the Easy BCR1? It's £50 all in, with no booking fee. See the Easy BCR1

1 Send your request Pick a time and send it to us on WhatsApp. You get a booking reference.
2 Pay by bank transfer Booked a live time? Pay straight away with the details shown and your reference. Requested a time? We check with the clinic first, then send you a payment link. You pay the clinic's price plus a £2 booking fee.
3 We book it for you As soon as you've paid, we pay the clinic and book the appointment in your name, so you get the clinic's own confirmation email.
4 If we can't book it Live times are checked 4 times a day, so a slot can occasionally go before we book it. If it has, we'll offer you the next time or refund you in full within 24 hours. Your choice.
Cancellations Once your appointment is booked, the clinic's own cancellation policy applies. You'll find it on each clinic above. If the clinic refunds or moves your booking, we pass that on to you in full. The £2 booking fee isn't refundable once your appointment is booked. Easy BCR1 bookings can be changed or cancelled free up to 48 hours before. Full details are in our booking terms.
